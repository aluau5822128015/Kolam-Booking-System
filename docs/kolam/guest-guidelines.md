# Guest Guidelines

## Shared apartment
Each flat is a 3BHK with three private bedrooms (King, Queen, Twin) and a shared Living Hall, Dining Area and Kitchen. Booking one bedroom means sharing the common areas with guests in the other bedrooms of the same flat. Booking the entire flat gives use of the whole flat.

## Food timings
| Service | Timing |
|---|---|
| Morning tea/coffee | 6:45 AM – 9:00 AM |
| Breakfast (complimentary, served in dining area, no parcel service) | 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM |
| Evening tea/coffee | 5:30 PM – 6:00 PM |
| Lunch | ₹250/meal — **currently unavailable** (cylinder issues) |
| Dinner | ₹250/meal — **currently unavailable** (cylinder issues) |

Breakfast menu: South Indian item + Tea/Coffee; optional Bread & Jam.

## Laundry
- Single room booking: ₹105 per load / 10 pieces, handled by the caretaker.
- Entire flat booking: free usage.
- Guests must use their own liquid detergent.

## Additional charges
Extra floor bed ₹560 (with bedsheet); lost room key ₹300; damage: towel ₹500, double bedsheet ₹750, comforter ₹1,300, mattress/linen ₹1,800. Early check-in ₹1,680 (with breakfast); late checkout ₹900 per 4 hours until 7 PM.

## Amenities (from official website)
Spacious rooms, kitchen access, meal options, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, parking, laundry, living room/common area, family-friendly, long-stay accommodation.
Wi-Fi credentials are shared with guests by staff and are intentionally not documented here.

## Guest instructions
Check-in 12:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM. Observe quiet hours and the caretaker/kitchen break. See `house-rules.md` and `booking-policy.md`.
